Codelumi · Support

Pickup-code organizer for iPhone

What Codelumi does

Codelumi keeps the pickup codes hidden in your delivery SMS in one place, so you do not have to scroll back through Messages at the locker. Everything is stored on your iPhone — there is no account, no server, and no upload.

Adding a code

  • Paste the whole delivery text into the Add sheet.
  • Shortcuts: build an automation that passes the SMS text to Codelumi; the parser runs on-device.
  • Screenshot: pick a screenshot of the message and Codelumi reads the code from the image locally.
