Allude · Support

Pickup-code organizer for iPhone

What Allude does

Allude keeps the pickup codes hidden in your delivery SMS in one place, so you do not have to scroll back through Messages at the locker. Everything is stored on your iPhone — there is no account, no server, and no upload.

Adding a code

  • Paste the whole delivery text into the Add sheet.
  • Shortcuts: build an automation that passes the SMS text to Allude; the parser runs on-device.
  • Screenshot: pick a screenshot of the message and Allude reads the code from the image locally.

Subscription and purchase

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Settings › Allude Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active until it ends. The lifetime purchase never renews.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data

Where are my records stored?

On this device only, inside the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them; we keep no copy.

Why does a code look wrong?

The parser reads text patterns, and couriers change their wording from time to time. You can always edit any field by hand. If a common format keeps failing, email us the message text with the numbers changed.
